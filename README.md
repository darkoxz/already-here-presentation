# Already Here

A 2-minute talk about technology and our future, told as five scenes of one animated world (the Titanic's iceberg: what we see on top is the future, what is already here sits below the waterline).

**Watch it:** open the live page and press *Start presentation*.

- Next scene: `→`, `Space`, `PageDown`, a presentation clicker, a click anywhere, or the **Next** button
- Back: `←`, `PageUp`
- `F` full screen · `M` sound · `N` speaker notes

Every scene and every sound effect was made in code. Each scene plays on its own and then waits for the presenter.

Sources: Gallup & Telescope (2025); AP (2026); GeekWire (2026); IDC Online (2026); Inbenta (2026); Trusted Reviews (2025).
